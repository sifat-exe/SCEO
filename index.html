Transparent Scoring System

Formula breakdown to guarantee fair comparison across different department sizes

1. Normalized Energy Reduction

Reduction % = (Baseline - Current) / Baseline × 100

Awards 25 points per 1.0% reduction. Uses normalized kWh per occupied room-hour so departments with more rooms or heavy lab sessions are not penalized.

2. Verified Action Completion

Action Points = Completed Actions × 50 pts

Awards 50 points for each practical energy initiative (e.g. Fume Hood sash campaigns, workstation sleep profiles, or chiller setbacks).

3. Participation & Pledge Rate

Participation Score = Participation % × 2.0

Awards up to 200 points based on faculty participation in energy audits, daylighting pledges, and staff engagement.

Configured Challenge Achievements:
⚡ Energy Saver Awarded for ≥ 7.0% verified reduction vs normalized baseline.
📈 Consistent Improver Awarded for steady reductions across 2+ consecutive evaluation periods.
🏆 Action Champion Awarded for completing 3 or more verified energy-saving actions.
